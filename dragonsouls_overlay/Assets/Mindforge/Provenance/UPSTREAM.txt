IMPORTANT ASSET BOUNDARY
------------------------
The upstream repository contains many external free art, animation, sound, music,
and Asset Store resources. The repository-level MIT license is treated as clear
permission for upstream-authored software/documentation, but Mindforge does not
assume that every bundled third-party binary asset is independently MIT-licensed.

The complete upstream checkout therefore remains local and git-ignored until each
asset family that we want to redistribute is separately audited or replaced with
known permissive/public-domain production art.
