# Lord Malatract Boss Spec

## Character rule

Lord Malatract believes he is the central villain of a severe, prestige grimdark epic. The game never tells him otherwise.

He is tall, highly articulated, symmetrical, controlled, and visually much more realistic than the Prism Squire. His humor comes entirely from maintaining cosmic seriousness while fighting a bright wobbling block knight.

## Visual language

- black titanium / obsidian armor;
- narrow violet control signals;
- restrained cyan only when Aetheria's energy is being stolen or redirected;
- articulated crown/warlock horns formed from hard-light geometry;
- long cloth/cable mantle with minimal flutter;
- precise animation, no squash-and-stretch;
- face or mask should remain unreadably calm.

## Arena

Use the existing enclosed arena district and outer ring. Do not create a void-edge boss platform.

The fight needs three elevation options:

- central floor for melee;
- side terraces / landing pockets for aerial recovery;
- broad outer lane that can later support a mounted phase without making a hoverbike mandatory for controller-only completion.

## Phase 1: Doctrine of Order

Goal: establish melee timing.

Attacks:

- **Edict Cut** — fast narrow blade check.
- **Royal Delay** — exaggerated delayed overhead intended to catch panic-rolls.
- **Ordered Sweep** — broad horizontal control attack.
- **Violet Decree** — single parryable gaze orb after a clear charge.

Rules:

- attacks commit before contact;
- tracking lock is visually obvious;
- recovery is punishable;
- no untelegraphed full-arena damage.

## Phase 2: Harmonic Suppression

Goal: combine focus interaction and movement.

Attacks:

- **Orb Canon** — sequence of 3 gaze orbs at changing cadence;
- **Silence Grid** — hard-light lanes appear, then become damaging after commitment;
- **Processional Lunge** — long committed approach that rewards lateral dodge;
- **Choir Break** — radial projectile pattern with safe sectors.

The first qualified focus-reflection setpiece belongs here. Controller-only mode must provide a deterministic fallback interaction for software/play qualification; live BCI qualification is a separate evidence path.

## Phase 3: The World Must Hold Still

Goal: test the entire movement language without feature soup.

- Malatract seals portions of the arena with ordered hard-light walls;
- outer lane opens as the safest fast route;
- optional Prism hoverbike becomes available for traversal/combat advantage;
- player may remain on foot and complete the fight;
- Malatract alternates heavy committed melee with spoke/ring pressure;
- final reflected orb should stagger him long enough for an Aetherblade finisher.

Mounted combat is an option, not a required gimmick. The boss never becomes a racing game.

## Dialogue structure

Keep transmissions short during gameplay. Longer monologues belong before/after phases or during non-damaging transitions.

Examples of tone, not final script:

- “Motion is error. Error is suffering. I will grant this realm the mercy of stillness.”
- “Your order is noise disguised as freedom.”
- “When the final signal resolves, there will be no uncertainty left to fear.”

Do not write joke responses for the Prism Squire. Let helmet wobble, bouncing, bike posture, and absurdly oversized sword carry the comedy.

## Defeat

Malatract should not explode into slapstick debris. His defeat should remain visually serious so the protagonist's physical celebration becomes the release.

Suggested beat:

1. armor control lights fail in perfect sequence;
2. crown hard-light fractures;
3. he kneels, still composed;
4. Prism Squire lands badly, bounces once, then raises the massive Aetherblade with excessive enthusiasm;
5. Aetheria's colored signals return across the skyline.

## Engineering constraints

- reuse existing boss authority wherever possible;
- no presentation script may own hit resolution;
- phase transitions may gate attack selection but may not write BCI evidence;
- VEP coded luminance remains isolated;
- mounted mode and foot mode are mutually exclusive locomotion authorities;
- controller-only and live-BCI qualifications remain separate.
