# Aetheria Player and Enemy Roster

## Prism Squire

### Read at distance

The Guardian should read as a small bright block body inside comically oversized holographic medieval armor. The helmet is a separate visual mass with secondary wobble. The Aetherblade is intentionally too large for the body carrying it.

### Combat identity

- fast three-cut Aetherblade chain;
- sword sweep can physically parry projectiles;
- dodge roll is primary grounded defense;
- one air dash per airtime;
- double jump + bounded hover create vertical combat routes;
- mounted Aetherblade preserves the same attack authority while changing locomotion rhythm;
- accepted Sight resonance may scale blade manifestation/reach only within bounded existing rules;
- future Stare of Defiance uses accepted focus to transform an already-valid defensive interaction, never to originate input.

### Demo weapon families

Do not build an inventory system yet. Preserve one control grammar and eventually expose three authored variants:

1. **Prism Broadsaber** — balanced baseline, current demo default.
2. **Momentum Lance** — longer committed thrust and stronger mounted identity.
3. **Twin Flickers** — short reach, faster aerial chaining.

V1 implements and polishes the Broadsaber/Aetherblade only.

## Cyber-Mythic Horde: ten combat identities

The ten-role Menagerie is the authoritative demo roster. Aetheria names/theme can coexist with existing internal archetype names until Unity play proves the presentation.

### 1. Scrap Goblin / Rift Hollow

- silhouette: low, forward, knife-heavy;
- lesson: target switching and crowd control;
- attacks: fast snap, wider hook;
- behavior comedy may include presentation-only stumble/skid, never random gameplay self-destruction in qualification mode.

### 2. Shardsinger

- silhouette: floating obelisk / tuning fork;
- lesson: close distance through ranged pressure;
- attacks: precision lance and fan/chord burst.

### 3. Bass Golem / Signal Warden

- silhouette: massive cathedral armor with obvious chest core;
- lesson: heavy commitment and parryable ranged pressure;
- attacks: judgement cleave and triune burst;
- future visual death gag: outer armor explodes away to reveal a tiny robotic frame, presentation-only after authoritative defeat.

### 4. Null Sentry

- silhouette: suspended keel + fins;
- lesson: tracking-lock timing;
- attacks: lockbolt, fan burst, breakaway pulse.

### 5. Chrome Penitent

- silhouette: executioner armor;
- lesson: stop panic-rolling;
- attacks: fast slash, delayed bell/overhead, wide sweep.

### 6. Rift Stalker / Rune Hound

- silhouette: low mantis predator;
- lesson: respect committed line attacks and lateral dodges;
- attacks: pounce lane and false-beat mixup;
- next mechanics pass should make the pounce body advance physically match the lane.

### 7. Choir Drone

- silhouette: floating core between tuning forks/halos;
- lesson: recognize projectile cadence under other pressure;
- attacks: tone, five-shot crescendo, recoil retreat.

### 8. Prism Maw

- silhouette: squat four-jaw creature with central eye;
- lesson: leave cones rather than rolling straight backward;
- attacks: mouth cone and precision needle.

### 9. Veil Reaper

- silhouette: extremely tall bifurcated executioner with scythe structures;
- lesson: fast-versus-delayed timing mixups;
- attacks: whisper, toll, horizon pressure.

### 10. Orbit Seraph

- silhouette: central machine orb with orthogonal halo structure;
- lesson: read the floor as a spoke pattern and reposition early;
- attacks: horizon fan and verdict shot.

## Encounter syntax

Enemy variety matters only when combinations produce readable questions.

Useful pairings:

- Goblin + Sentry: close pressure while a ranged lock develops.
- Penitent + Shardsinger: delayed melee timing under high-lane pressure.
- Stalker + Choir: lateral lane dodge followed by projectile rhythm.
- Maw + Reaper: cone-space denial plus delayed execution timing.
- Warden + Seraph: heavy anchor plus radial reposition pressure.

Avoid combinations where four attacks use the same color, geometry, and lock timing simultaneously.

## Visibility hierarchy

1. immediate damaging threat;
2. enemy commitment lock;
3. player health/endurance state;
4. target lock and active enemy silhouette;
5. Aetherblade contact trail;
6. world landmarks;
7. ambient neon and decorative motion.

Bright world art must never outrank a danger telegraph.
